# Products of theorizing

This section examines the various products of theorizing that come into play before, during, and after the theorizing process.

Here are some topics that are in need of a contribution to specify their role and importance in theorizing:
- Research questions
- Myths
- Analogies
- Metaphors
- Concepts
- Constructs/variables
- Conjectures/propositions/hypothesis
- Models
- Frameworks
