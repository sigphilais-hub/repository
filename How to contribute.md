# How to contribute

The SIGPhil Repository is conceived as a collaborative and evolving resource, and contributions from the community are both welcomed and encouraged. Scholars, students, and practitioners who wish to enrich the repository with relevant explanations, materials, reflections, case examples, or curated resources are invited to do so.

To ensure coherence, quality, and transparency, contributions should follow the submission procedure outlined below. This procedure specifies the format, scope, and review process for proposed additions. By adhering to these guidelines, contributors help maintain the repository’s academic rigor while supporting its openness and pluralistic spirit. Adhering to the established submission procedure also significantly supports the work of the repository’s moderators. As members of the community who balance this initiative alongside demanding academic and professional responsibilities, moderators rely on clear, structured submissions to review and integrate contributions efficiently. Following the outlined process thus helps minimize delays, reduces the need for additional clarification, and enables the repository to evolve in a timely and sustainable manner.
 
## Contribution Procedure

To ensure coherence, relevance, and efficient coordination, contributors are invited to follow the procedure outlined below:

**1. Initial Contact**  
Prospective contributors should first contact the SIGPhil group to discuss their proposed contribution. This step helps ensure the relevance of the topic to the repository’s scope and provides an opportunity to identify potential collaborations if multiple contributors are interested in working on the same or closely related topics.

**2. Preparation of the Contribution**  
Once the topic has been confirmed, contributors are invited to develop their submission in a Microsoft Word document. The document should be as simple as possible and follow formatting and structural guidelines provided by the repository's moderators.

**3. Submission to the Moderators**  
The completed document should be sent to the repository moderators. Upon receipt, the contribution will be reviewed, compiled as needed, and subsequently uploaded to the platform.
