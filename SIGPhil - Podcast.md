# SIGPhil - Podcast

TThe SIGPhil Podcast offers a space for engaging discussions on theorizing and philosophical issues in the Information Systems (IS) field. Through conversations with scholars and practitioners, it explores how theories are developed, challenged, and applied, while also addressing broader philosophical questions that underpin IS research and practice.

Below, you will find a collection of podcast episodes covering a range of topics related to theory, theorizing, and philosophy in IS. As an evolving resource, this series aims to stimulate reflection, encourage dialogue, and support the community in strengthening its engagement with the theoretical and philosophical foundations of the field.
