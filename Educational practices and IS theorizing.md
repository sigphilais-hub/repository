# Educational practices and IS theorizing

The development of theorizing capabilities is shaped not only by research activities but also by educational practices that influence how students and scholars learn to engage with theory. This section is dedicated to resources, pedagogical approaches, and teaching experiences related to theorizing in the Information Systems (IS) field. It aims to support educators and learners by highlighting methods, tools, exercises, and educational reflections that foster the development of theoretical thinking and theorizing skills. By bringing together diverse teaching practices and perspectives, this section seeks to contribute to ongoing discussions about how theory and theorizing can be effectively taught, learned, and cultivated within the IS community.
